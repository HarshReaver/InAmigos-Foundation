Why This Feature Matters

A dedicated donation section with clear amounts and progress tracking builds trust and encourages visitors to contribute.

A
Pre-set Amount Buttons

Quick selection removes friction. Visitors don't have to think about how much to give.

B
Cause-Specific Donation

Donors can choose which project their money goes to — Seva, Bachpanshala, Jeev, etc.

C
Progress Bar

Shows how close the goal is. Creates urgency and social proof ("142 donors already contributed").

D
Trust Badges

Secure payment, 80G tax benefit, and Razorpay protection reassure donors their money is safe.

Current Gap on InAmigos Website:

The Donate button in the navigation is commented out (disabled). There is no dedicated donation page with progress tracking or cause selection.

Why This Feature Matters

A built-in volunteer form on the website removes the extra step of redirecting to Google Forms and keeps the experience seamless.

A
Simple Form Fields

Name, email, phone, and city — only the essentials. Long forms discourage signups.

B
Project Interest Checkboxes

Volunteers can pick which projects they care about, helping the team assign them effectively.

C
Motivation Field

Helps the team understand the volunteer's intent and filter serious applicants.

Current Gap on InAmigos Website:

The volunteer signup currently redirects to an external Google Form. A native form on the website would feel more professional and reduce drop-offs.

Why This Feature Matters

Impact numbers are the strongest proof of an NGO's work. Showing them visually builds credibility and trust with donors and visitors.

A
Stat Cards with Icons

Each project's impact shown separately with a clear number, label, and icon for quick scanning.

B
Progress Bars

Visual bars show progress toward goals, making the data feel tangible and motivating.

C
Geographic Reach

A map or reach indicator showing 28-state coverage highlights the scale of operations.

Current Gap on InAmigos Website:

The homepage mentions impact numbers in a text paragraph, but there's no visual dashboard or dedicated section to showcase them effectively.

Why This Feature Matters

Real stories from real people are the most powerful form of social proof. They humanize the NGO and convert visitors into supporters.

A
Three Perspectives

Volunteer, beneficiary, and donor — covering all types of stakeholders builds complete trust.

B
Real Names & Roles

Attributing quotes to named people with their role makes testimonials feel genuine, not fabricated.

C
Specific Details

Mentioning specific projects (Jeev, Bachpanshala) makes stories concrete rather than vague praise.

Current Gap on InAmigos Website:

There is no testimonial or success story section on the current website. Adding real stories would significantly boost credibility.

Why This Feature Matters

Campaigns create urgency and give visitors a specific, time-bound action to take — donate, volunteer, or share.

A
Status Badges

"Ongoing" and "Upcoming" badges let visitors instantly see what's active and what's coming next.

B
Campaign-Level Progress

Each campaign has its own goal and progress bar, so donors see exactly what their money achieves.

C
Dual CTAs

"Donate" and "Share" buttons on every campaign card — two ways to contribute, even without money.

Current Gap on InAmigos Website:

The Events page exists but campaigns/causes page is empty. There's no way to donate to or register for a specific campaign directly from the website.

Summary of Recommended Features

5 features that can be implemented on the InAmigos Foundation website to improve user experience, increase donations, and build trust.

Pre-set amounts, cause selection, progress tracking, and trust badges.

02

Built-in registration with project interest checkboxes instead of external Google Forms.

03

Impact Dashboard

Visual stat cards with numbers, icons, and progress bars showing real impact.

04

Testimonials

Real stories from volunteers, donors, and beneficiaries for social proof.

05

Campaign Highlights

Active campaigns with goals, progress, and direct donate/register buttons.
