Who We Are

Get to Know InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization, licensed by the Central Government, headquartered in Chhattisgarh, India.

We believe in the power of collective action. Every contribution goes towards food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, and social inclusion programmes across India.
