FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What data sources does NexusAI support?
We support 200+ integrations including PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Snowflake, BigQuery, Google Sheets, REST APIs, S3, and more. You can also upload CSV/Excel files directly.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We're SOC 2 Type II certified with end-to-end AES-256 encryption, SSO support, role-based access control, and full audit logs. Your data never leaves your region.
Can I try NexusAI before buying?
Yes! Our Starter plan is completely free with 1,000 queries per month. Pro plans come with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required.
Do I need technical skills to use it?
Not at all. NexusAI is built for everyone — just type your question in plain English like "What were our top products last month?" and the AI handles the query, analysis, and visualisation for you.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your dashboard.
